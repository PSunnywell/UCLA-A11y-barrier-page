Report an Accessibility Barrier

Help UCLA identify and address physical or digital barriers that may limit access to university spaces, programs, services, or information. You may report a barrier you experienced or one affecting someone else.

What should I report? Physical barriers might include inaccessible entrances, elevators, paths, or event spaces. Digital barriers might include websites that cannot be used with a keyboard, documents that do not work with screen readers, or videos without captions.

For immediate hazards or emergencies, use the appropriate emergency or campus response service rather than this form. For password resets and ordinary technical problems, contact your usual IT support service.
