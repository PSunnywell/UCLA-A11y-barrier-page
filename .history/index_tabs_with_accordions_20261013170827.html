Building a More Accessible Community

At UCLA, accessibility is essential to creating a community where everyone feels welcome, respected, and empowered to participate fully.

Guided by our True Bruin Value of Belonging, we strive to build an inclusive environment that recognizes and respects the diverse identities, experiences, and abilities of our campus community. Accessibility goes beyond removing barriers and meeting legal requirements. It is about creating meaningful opportunities for everyone to learn, work, connect, and thrive.

Through our shared commitment to accessibility, we help build a UCLA where everyone has the opportunity to participate, contribute, and belong.

Laws & Regulations

Federal and state requirements, including the ADA and Section 504, establish rights and obligations related to equal access and nondiscrimination.

UC Policies

University of California policies describe institutional expectations, responsibilities, and procedures. Check each policy for its scope and applicability.

Standards

Technical criteria, such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), help teams design and evaluate accessible experiences. Applicable requirements determine which standards are mandatory.
